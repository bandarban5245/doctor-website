Dr. Ahsan Rahman is a highly experienced Medicine Specialist dedicated to providing comprehensive healthcare with a patient-first approach. With over 15 years of clinical experience, he has helped thousands of patients manage complex medical conditions.

He specializes in internal medicine, diabetes management, hypertension, and preventive healthcare. His philosophy is simple: treat the person, not just the disease.
